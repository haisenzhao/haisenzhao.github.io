中文简介

赵海森，山东大学计算机科学与技术学院教授、博士生导师，山东大学杰出中青年人才、国家级青年人才。主要研究计算机图形学、数字几何设计，以及人工智能驱动的几何设计与制造。2018年获山东大学工学博士学位，导师为陈宝权教授；2019—2022年先后在美国华盛顿大学和奥地利科学技术研究所从事博士后研究，合作导师为 Adriana Schulz 教授和 Bernd Bickel 教授。

在 ACM TOG、ACM SIGGRAPH / SIGGRAPH Asia、IEEE TVCG、IROS 等期刊和会议发表多篇论文，出版专著1部，申请国家发明专利十余项和美国发明专利1项。主持国家自然科学基金优秀青年科学基金项目（海外）、国家重点研发计划课题、国家自然科学基金面上项目等，担任 CCF CAD&CG 专委会等多个学术组织委员。曾获山东省自然科学奖一等奖、CCF 优秀博士学位论文奖等。

Prospective students and researchers: I welcome inquiries from research interns, master’s and Ph.D. students, and postdoctoral researchers interested in geometric computing and intelligent manufacturing. Email me.

Research

My group studies computational methods across geometric design, analysis, and fabrication.

Geometric and Physical Intelligence

Geometric learning, generative design, microstructures, and performance-driven optimization.

Manufacturability and Process Planning

Accessibility analysis, decomposition, packing, process sequencing, and geometric verification.

Computational Fabrication

Toolpath optimization, additive and subtractive manufacturing, hybrid manufacturing, and robotic fabrication.

Academic background

Haisen Zhao is a Professor at Shandong University and a member of the Interdisciplinary Research Center. He received his Ph.D. from Shandong University and conducted postdoctoral research at the University of Washington and IST Austria.
